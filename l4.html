Lesson 4 – Customer Segmentation

Learning Outcomes

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Define and explain the concept of Customer Segmentation.
  • Identify the main types of customer segmentation used in businesses.
  • Understand how segmentation improves marketing and customer relationships.
  • Apply segmentation strategies to real-world business examples.
  • Recognize the importance of grouping customers for efficient resource use.

Introduction

Not all customers are the same. They have different needs, interests, and purchasing habits. Because of this, businesses must divide customers into smaller, more manageable groups that share common characteristics. This process is called Customer Segmentation.

Common Types of Customer Segmentation

TypeDescriptionExample
DemographicAge, gender, income, educationTeens, professionals, retirees
GeographicBased on locationUrban vs. rural areas
PsychographicLifestyle, interests, valuesEco-friendly buyers
BehavioralBuying habits, frequency, loyaltyRegular vs. one-time buyers

🧠 Quick Break Question

Which type of customer segmentation focuses on lifestyle, interests, and personal values?

  • Demographic Segmentation

  • Psychographic Segmentation

Summary

Customer Segmentation divides customers into smaller groups based on shared traits such as demographics, geography, psychographics, or behavior. This allows businesses to create personalized strategies, improve customer satisfaction, and use resources more effectively.
