Lesson 3 – Customer Analytics Process

Learning Outcomes

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Define and explain the Customer Analytics Process.
  • Describe each step involved in analyzing customer data.
  • Identify different tools used in Customer Analytics.
  • Apply the process to real-life business scenarios.
  • Understand how following the process leads to better decisions and customer satisfaction.

Introduction

The Customer Analytics Process is a systematic approach that allows organizations to move from collecting raw data to making informed business decisions. It helps companies identify patterns, predict behavior, and improve strategies based on facts rather than assumptions.

The Five-Step Customer Analytics Process

StepDescriptionExample
1. Data CollectionGather data from various sources.Websites, surveys, and social media interactions.
2. Data CleaningRemove duplicates or incomplete records.Fixing errors and removing missing values.
3. Data AnalysisUse tools to identify trends and patterns.Finding out which product sells best or when customers visit most.
4. Interpretation of ResultsUnderstand what the data means.Noting that students buy milk tea after school hours.
5. Decision-Making & ActionApply findings to business strategies.Launch “Student Happy Hour” to increase sales.

Tools Used in Customer Analytics

Tool TypeExamplesPurpose
SpreadsheetsMicrosoft Excel, Google SheetsBasic data organization and charts.
Visualization ToolsPower BI, TableauCreate visual dashboards.
CRM SystemsSalesforce, Zoho CRMTrack and manage customer interactions.
Web AnalyticsGoogle AnalyticsMonitor user activity and engagement online.

Summary

The Customer Analytics Process transforms raw data into actionable insights. By consistently applying this five-step process — collecting, cleaning, analyzing, interpreting, and acting — businesses can make smarter, data-driven decisions and improve customer satisfaction.
