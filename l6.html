Lesson 6 – Ethical Use of Customer Data and Privacy

Learning Outcomes

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Define and explain Data Privacy and its role in Customer Analytics.
  • Understand why protecting customer data is essential for trust and ethics.
  • Identify common data privacy issues faced by businesses.
  • Recognize ways to protect customer data using security and legal measures.
  • Apply ethical guidelines when handling customer information.

Introduction

Customer Analytics depends on data to understand consumer behavior. However, businesses must handle that data responsibly. Misuse of data can lead to loss of trust, penalties, and reputation damage. Ethical handling ensures trust, transparency, and long-term loyalty.

What is Data Privacy?

Data Privacy means protecting a customer’s personal information from unauthorized use or access. It gives customers control over how their data is collected and shared.

🧠 Quick Break Question 1

Which of the following best defines Data Privacy?

  • Using customer information for marketing without permission.
  • Protecting a customer’s personal information from unauthorized access.
  • Sharing data with other businesses to increase sales.
  • Collecting as much personal information as possible.

Why is Data Privacy Important?

ReasonExplanation
Protects CustomersPrevents misuse of personal information and identity theft.
Builds TrustCustomers feel secure sharing data when they know it’s safe.
Avoids Legal IssuesBusinesses must comply with privacy laws such as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
Improves Business ImageEthical data handling makes a company appear responsible and credible.

Ethical Guidelines in Customer Analytics

  • Obtain customer consent before collecting data.
  • Use data only for its intended purpose.
  • Be transparent about how data will be used.
  • Delete or secure unnecessary data.
  • Never sell or share customer data without permission.

🧠 Quick Break Question 2

Which of the following practices is considered ethical in handling customer data?

  • Selling customer contact information to third-party advertisers.
  • Sending emails to customers without their consent.
  • Securing customer information and using it only with their permission.
  • Ignoring data protection laws to speed up analysis.

Summary

Customer Analytics must always follow ethical and legal guidelines. Protecting customer data through responsible practices builds trust and ensures compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Ethical handling of data promotes transparency, accountability, and sustainable business success.
